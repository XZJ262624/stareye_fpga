# Scope and limits

This change adds passive, candidate-weighted shadow statistics. The original whole-row production promotion decision is unchanged. Counts are opportunities under hypothetical finer matching, not a new display algorithm or measured image-quality improvement.

The seven u32 fields count P0 candidate events. Complete/tag/equality rejection counts can overlap. Strong-loss and illegal-class flags are whole-row risks; there is no joint shadow-pass × risk distribution. They do not yield a false-positive rate. Ratios require a complete, non-aborted, non-saturated record with P0 greater than zero. A zero-P0 record provides no strategy-benefit estimate.

Production CE is constant one. Qualified CE-pause tests cover bank/pipeline freezing and explicitly accepted observer events, not arbitrary held-DE feeding. Full-width identity/cookie and original TTL/config/lease/tag protections remain required. No semantic matching protection is removed.

The enabled system uses conditional 1M request cadence and accelerated UART_CLOCK_HZ=960000 with BAUD=460800. Its actual watchdog is four simulated seconds; the historical preparation text saying two seconds is corrected by SYSTEM_WATCHDOG_METADATA_RECONCILIATION.json without changing the executed inputs. Separate default-rate finite packet proofs do not establish default full-frame throughput. At 460800 baud, 8N1, the first 345600-byte class/mask export needs at least 7.5 seconds for payload alone, exceeding the approximately two-second source TTL. Later fresh publication may be necessary; this is arithmetic, not a measured startup guarantee.

Current resource use is 51321/51680 permitted XLR including 2150 routing, 196/204 RAM and 1/128 DSP. Only 359 XLR and eight RAM remain. This narrow positive result is not cross-PVT, hardware, hot-reset or warm-recovery qualification. External input/output delay boundaries are seven/135; internal no-clock/unconstrained/multiple-clock are zero/zero/zero.
